# LeakLens — Final Submission Pitch

## 1. 60-Second Elevator Pitch

> **[Problem]**  
> *"Online merchants process thousands of customer transactions every day, but payment captures, settlement payouts, refund deductions, and gateway fees live in disconnected records that frequently disagree."*  
>  
> **[Pain]**  
> *"Reconciling these records manually in spreadsheets is slow, error-prone, and hides revenue leakage — missing payouts, duplicate fees, and delayed settlements go unnoticed for weeks."*  
>  
> **[Solution]**  
> *"LeakLens solves this by creating a deterministic financial truth layer first. Using exact Python Decimal arithmetic, it reconciles records in milliseconds and classifies 7 exact exception types down to the exact paisa."*  
>  
> **[AI & Trust]**  
> *"Then, Evidence-Grounded AI investigates those discrepancies. The key principle is that AI never calculates or alters financial truth — it interprets verified ledger evidence to explain what happened and suggest safe next steps."*  
>  
> **[Impact]**  
> *"With an interactive Action Center and publication-ready PDF reports, LeakLens turns hours of manual spreadsheet auditing into seconds of high-impact financial clarity."*

---

## 2. 30-Second Concise Pitch

> *"LeakLens is an AI-assisted financial reconciliation platform for online merchants. It compares payments, settlements, refunds, and fees using deterministic financial rules, identifies discrepancies and their exact monetary impact, and uses evidence-grounded AI to explain root causes.  
>  
> The core architectural principle is that AI never determines financial truth — it empowers finance teams to investigate and resolve issues with complete transparency and trust."*
